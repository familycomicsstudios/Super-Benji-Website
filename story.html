Part 1: The Adventures of Benji

Chapter 1: Super Benji Adventures (Super Benji Movie)

Act 1

Dr. Banana steals the Banana Gem from Benji, and brings it up to his secret base to use it to power a superweapon with the powers to destroy the world. Benji goes up to Dr. Banana’s secret base to try to get it back but Dr. Banana opens a trapdoor beneath him and he falls back to earth. He goes back to the League of Grace and tells them what happened. Dr. Banana delivers a poisoned pizza to the League of Grace and Hallie eats it and gets sick. Noah says that he put a tracking device on Dr. Banana. They have Dr. Zach and Dr. Jack help Hallie, and Benji, Jordan and Noah go to find Dr. Banana.

They go on their sleds to Benji City to go find Dr. Banana, but while they do so Jack calls them and says they found out the pizza was poisoned. They try to tell Benji what Dr. Banana’s plan was but the call cuts out. Tempter gets sent by Dr. Banana to fight Benji and crew but he loses. Scarlette joins them to help. Later, Dr. Banana shows Tempter the Bananabot 2.0 which is allegedly the same as the Bananabot 1.0. Benji and crew get to Benji City and they go to Wisconsin in a Building where Benji tries to order beer but he’s only 4 years old. Emmy comes to them and tells them that Addie got kidnapped by Dr. Banana.

Act 2 Part 1

Dr. Banana also kidnaps Addie and takes her to one of his secret bases. Addie is shown to be getting dangled over lava and Dr. Banana reveals that they’re trying to use Addie as bait to lure the League of Grace so that the Banana Republic can defeat them. Dr. Banana sets a 24 hour timer that lowers Addie into the lava. Emmy joins them and they keep going towards Dr. Banana. Jack tries to become Tinfoil Man by putting tinfoil on his head, then he gets struck by lightning and teleports for a bit to talk to Benji and crew.

Dr. Banana then sends Baby Stoplight to attack Benji and crew and Benji looks Baby Stoplight in his red light and gets zapped. They ask a random bystander for help and he says “I Like Trains” and Baby Stoplight and the I Like Trains Guy get hit by a train. Dr. Banana and Saber find out where Benji and crew are and send a nuclear bomb straight to their location. Benji and crew get hit by the nuclear bomb, and Dr. Banana and Saber see the blast radius and not Benji and crew. Benji and crew hid in a hole to survive the nuclear bomb. Zach also joins the party. Meanwhile, Dr. Banana sends Jack a bribe of 10 million dollars plus more to be a spy for him and tell him lots of information about Benji.

Act 2 Part 2

Benji and crew climb Chestnut Mountain. They go to the bottom of Chestnut Mountain and fight Bobby Banana, Dr. Banana’s son, to get past the Prison house, which Bobby Banana is guarding. They get into the prison house and then fight Tempter. Tempter beats Benji and crew but Scarlette beats up the Tempter after everybody else gets kicked out. They go to the Acorn Village and also into the Wisconsin in a Building in the village. The Photograpper, another one of Dr. Banana’s minions, tells the bartender that Benji doesn’t pay his taxes (which Benji confesses to by accident) and Benji gets sent to jail. Meanwhile Jack and Hallie come to bail out Benji. Meanwhile in Banana City Dr. Banana declares himself Emperor of the Universe.

Dr. Banana receives a letter from Benji and crew which contains Bobby Banana’s dead body. Benji and crew go across Grace Bay. Meanwhile Addie escapes. Tempter and Saber, who are guarding her, are wearing human masks to disguise themselves so Addie doesn’t attack them, but Addie attacks them and escapes. They go into Lake GenevaThe Grace Bay and fight Baby Stoplight again as well as his kids. They beat all of them up. Addie goes to Hallie Village, which is near Banana City, and asks where Benji and crew are. The crew then goes to The Crystal Caves.

Baby Stoplight invades Hallie Village, but Benji and crew show up and they fight Baby Stoplight again. Dr. Banana’s minion, Minion, tells Dr. Banana that Baby Stoplight lost twice and failed to stop them. Dr. Banana goes to Benji City. They have the Bananabot protect the castle too. Dr. Banana steals a bunch of nuclear bombs from Benji’s base. Scarlette also goes missing. Scarlette then comes back a bit later to tell them the Bananabot is coming back to beat up the crew. Dr. Banana pilots the Bananabot to beat everyone up. But Benji’s crew beats up Dr. Banana and the Bananabot. The Bananabot ends up in critical condition and it can’t fight anymore. Benji arrives in, and then burns down a bunch of stuff, in Banana City.

Act 2 Part 3

Dr. Banana sends out the Bananabot again, even though it was in critical condition. Benji declares war on Dr. Banana for stealing his nukes. They fight the Bananabot again, this time without Dr. Banana controlling it. Benji and the Bananabot insult each other. While they fight Dr. Banana sends one of Benji’s nukes towards Benji City. It lands in the Benji City Clearing and hits Jordan, who got hit all the way back to Benji City while they fought the Bananabot. People in Benji City riot to ban superheroes because they think it was Benji who nuked them due to the fact it was one of Benji’s nukes. Mayor Labelle finds out that it was actually Dr. Banana because he stole Benji’s nukes.

At Dr. Banana’s castle everyone fights Dr. Banana, and Jordan comes back to help. Dr. Banana gets put under arrest by one of Benji City’s soldiers. But for some reason Dr. Banana is reported dead, and everyone celebrates, including the Benji City Council, who try to charge Jordan for accidental manslaughter for killing Dr. Banana. Mayor Labelle parades through Benji City now that Dr. Banana is dead but they get nuked and assassinated by one of Dr. Banana’s minions. Meanwhile Benji and crew find out Dr. Banana’s body is missing. They find a letter from Dr. Banana that says he faked his death. They look at Dr. Banana’s search history to find where he went and discover he left for Buttburner.

Act 3

Benji and crew go on a spaceship heading straight for Buttburner. However it crash lands on the Lightning Planet. They fight The Photograpper there and he gets defeated. Dr. Banana makes it to Buttburner and hides away in his castle. They head to another rocket ship across the planet. Meanwhile on the ship Benji makes a band called BenjiBeatz. Meanwhile Triangle Man tries to cook a pizza on the rope where Addie was, and the timer ends and the pizza explodes. Triangle Man then dies. Benji’s ship crashes again on Cookie Island. For some reason they meet various Veggietales characters. But a Hallie lands on them and kills them.

Meanwhile, Dr. Banana tracks Benji’s current location and launches a nuke at them. They get back on the ship and make another BenjiBeatz song because the first one got #1 on the charts (but they got sued cause it was a cover song). They go #1 again, but get sued again for making their song the same as another one. They get to Buttburner but get shot out of the sky. A guy makes them tell a Yo Mama joke to enter Buttburner, and they manage to get in. They go to Buttburner City, which they discover has a lot of industrial businesses. Jacob, a shadowy figure, seemingly helps them get to Dr. Banana’s castle on Buttburner, but instead of helping he is revealed to be Dr. Banana’s spy, and throws everyone into a volcano.

Hallie turns big and everyone rides on her to survive the volcano as the lava floats up. They find a secret camera Dr. Banana placed. They head back up to Dr. Banana’s castle and Dr. Banana sends Jacob to attack them. Scarlette attacks Jacob and beats him up. They get to Dr. Banana’s castle. Benji flies into Dr. Banana’s spaceship as Benji and Dr. Banana have an epic battle. Benji steals the Banana Gem back and flies out of Dr. Banana’s spaceship. They have another epic battle with Dr. Banana and defeat him. Benji discovers that cantaloupes grant him flight.
Everyone gets back down to earth and Jean, the grandmother of the League of Grace and one of the founders of the League of Grace, congratulates them all for saving the world by returning the Banana Gem back to the League of Grace. BenjiBeatz makes another rap song to celebrate. They find out that Dr. Banana nuked Benji’s base as a last resort and it’s almost destroyed. Jonah calls the League of Grace to get them ready to train for another fight…

Chapter 2: Civil War (Super Benji Movie 2)

Act 1

Benji dreams about Dr. Banana’s troops invading Benji’s base. In the dream Dr. Banana and his army march into Benji’s base, but as they’re about to reach it Benji wakes up screaming. Benji goes outside and sees Dr. Banana actually outside of Benji’s base. Benji talks to Dr. Banana and Dr. Banana talks about a treaty to not attack Benji for now. Benji tells the League of Grace he needs to go to a Benji City meeting to restore their government after Mayor Labelle’s death, and Benji says he’s going to try and rule Benji City legally. Meanwhile, Jordan and Noah and Tempter and Saber are all gambling at a casino. Noah and Jordan try to cheat with mirror glasses but they realize that the glasses actually show Tempter and Saber their cards instead of letting them see theirs. Scarlette tries to help Noah and Jordan escape by threatening to shoot everyone in the casino. Tempter calls 911 and tells them that Scarlette is threatening to shoot everyone. The 911 operator figures out that Tempter is a Banana Republic member and says he doesn’t get paid enough to help and says Tempter and Saber can do whatever they want. Scarlette shoots Tempter and Saber and Noah and Jordan and Scarlette go home.

Back at League of Grace HQ, everyone says that if the treaty doesn’t work then Dr. Banana will attack Benji for sure. Scarlette suggests that if Dr. Banana doesn’t sign the treaty, they’ll all attack Dr. Banana, and everyone agrees. The League of Grace gets mailed a video from Jonah, their cousin and a Benji City diplomat, who says that they’ve trained a new member of the League of Grace, a dog named Chloe. Chloe starts talking to everyone by barking and everyone seems to understand her. Meanwhile, Benji meets with the Benji City Council, their three cousins Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah. Benji gets a call from Dr. Banana where he says that he’s declining the treaty, so Benji declares war on Dr. Banana on behalf of Benji City. Meanwhile, Jacob tries to assassinate Benji but can’t bring himself to fire. The council tells Benji that they can’t let him go to war against Dr. Banana because it’ll ruin Benji City’s uncorrupted politics as well as the economy, and they throw Benji out of the window. Benji gets fed up with Benji City’s alleged corrupt government, and starts planning to overthrow the government in a dictatorship that Benji runs.

The council throws a party now that Benji’s gone, but Jacob shoots Eli instead of Benji and Eli gets taken to the hospital. Now that Eli leaves, Benji goes into the Council building to try and overthrow it. Benji fights two guards to get back into the building, and then declares himself emperor of Benji City. He calls Scarlette, who asks him why he overthrew the government if both Benji and the government were both capitalist, and Benji responds by saying that he was upset the government wouldn’t let him go to war. Benji makes a public appearance as emperor. His first order of business is to declare war on Dr. Banana, and his second order of business is to remove freedom of speech and he declares severe punishment on anyone speaking negatively of him or positively of Dr. Banana. An audience member tells Benji he sucks and Benji arrests him. On 9:01 News they say that Benji overthrew the government and that Benji City is now a capitalist monarchy.

Act 2

Dr. Banana and Minion discuss that Benji is now the emperor, and that Benji plans to declare war on him. Dr. Banana discovers he’s vastly outnumbered, but Minion tells him a genius plan: to recruit League of Grace members to his side to balance the odds. Benji goes to Wisconsin in a Building and Baby Stoplight, disguised as a bartender, annoys Benji so much that he leaves. The League of Grace meets at the Benji City Council and they decide on new laws to implement. Emmy suggests new police officers, because Benji hasn’t been doing an amazing job of stopping crime. Scarlette suggests making Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah diplomats for Benji City. Benji calls Dr. Banana and tells him to sign the treaty or Benji will nuke him. Dr. Banana invites Benji to his castle to discuss the treaty, and Benji comes to meet with him. They discuss some terms, including that their militaries won’t fight each other and that they won’t nuke each other except in self defense. Both of them sign the treaty.

Dr. Banana and Benji make a public appearance in Benji City. Dr. Banana says that Benji’s been treating his citizens unfairly and says that his unfair treatment ends now that they’re in a treaty. While they talk a nuke flies above their head. Dr. Banana says it wasn’t one of his, and they start fighting. Meanwhile, Jacob says that Dr. Banana backstabbed him and Benji doesn’t like him, but Jordan comes to talk with him. Jacob asks Jordan to share his secrets, and Jordan asks for 3 million dollars and a supercar. Jacob takes the offer and Jordan reveals Benji is going to backstab Dr. Banana now that they have a treaty, as well as that he’s going to expand his empire all the way across Benji Island, and limit everyone’s free speech. Jonah and Isaiah meet Eli in the hospital, who is recovering quickly. In the hospital they watch 9:01 News, which reveals that Dr. Banana and Benji have sealed their alliance, but a nuclear bomb hit Benji City while they were talking. The news also says that the nuke wasn’t either Benji’s nor Dr. Banana’s.

Benji and Dr. Banana talk outside of the Council building, and Dr. Banana lets it slip that he wants to turn Benji City into a communist city. Benji and Dr. Banana kick the rest of the League of Grace out of the Council and they discuss laws to make, which include ones that say that Dr. Banana’s supporters get thrown in jail. Benji tries to ban the letter B because B is for Banana but he doesn’t realize that Benji starts with B as well. Jacob and Minion, at Dr. Banana’s castle, fire another nuke at Benji City, and it hits Eli as he walks out of the hospital. Benji and Noah test their new nuclear bomb on Banana City but they fire the wrong bomb, and the one they fired was a confetti one. Dr. Banana sneaks into Benji’s base to try and find the nuclear launch codes, but Chloe stops him. 9:01 News reveals that Dr. Banana and Benji have both been breaking their treaty, because Dr. Banana snuck into Benji’s base, and because Benji launched a nuke at Banana City. Benji tells Dr. Banana that their alliance probably won’t work, so they break up the alliance under one condition. Dr. Banana and Benji talk to the rest of the League of Grace, and Dr. Banana says that anyone who wants to stay on Benji’s side can go to the left side of the room, and anyone who wants to join him can go to the right side. But Dr. Banana turns off the lights in the room, and Dr. Banana tricks everyone into having Jordan, Noah, Scarlette, and Hallie join his team because they accidentally walked to his side.

Act 3

Dr. Banana and his new allies go back to his base, and Benji and the rest of the League of Grace - Addie, Emmy, Zach, and Jack - go back to the Council. Benji says that each member of his team, including the Council, gets to train their own army to help fight Dr. Banana. Dr. Banana talks with Noah and Jordan, and they talk to him about new ideas for bombs Dr. Banana can make. They show him the Bananabomb, which would recycle the Bananabot and turn it into a new bomb. Dr. Banana says that he doesn’t want them to recycle the Bananabot, but Noah and Jordan tell Dr. Banana that they already made the bomb. Dr. Banana wants to use it immediately to nuke Benji City, but Noah and Jordan tell him it’ll destroy the whole world if they use it. Minion tells Scarlette and Hallie that the Banana Republic is broke, and it’s because Scarlette and Hallie ate a lot of cheese curds with the Bananas’ credit card. Scarlette and Hallie tell Minion they convinced Eli and his army to switch to Dr. Banana’s side.

9:01 News says that Dr. Banana sends Eli’s army to attack Benji City first. Zach and Jonah’s army fights one of Dr. Banana’s armies, and Zach tells Jonah about the Bananabomb. Jonah tells Zach that him and Jack should turn into Dr. Zach and Jack and go look for the Bananabomb so they can find out how to recreate it, but Zach reveals that Noah and Jordan were the ones who made it. Dr. Banana’s army uses fart bombs against Isaiah’s army, and he calls Benji to ask for help. Benji tells him that he has the advantage and he should keep fighting, but Isaiah says that his army sucks and doesn’t know how to fight. So Benji starts a draft in Benji City so they can get more good soldiers. Isaiah disagrees, so Benji says that he’s on his own. Jonah and Isaiah coordinate to do an assault on Dr. Banana’s army as a diversion, but then use tanks once Dr. Banana’s army is distracted. They start doing the plan, and Dr. Zach and Jack use a tank to attack, but it gets stuck in the mud. They see Eli, who joined Dr. Banana’s side, and Eli reveals he’s getting paid more by Dr. Banana. Dr. Zach and Jack fight Eli, but he doesn’t have powers, so he uses his supersuit to get the upper hand in the fight. But his suit hadn’t been used since 2009 so it takes a while to start up. Dr. Zach and Jack fight Eli in his supersuit. While Eli fights, he accidentally throws Zach and Jack into Dr. Banana’s castle.

Meanwhile, Benji and Emmy campaign in Benji City, but when Benji says that the League of Grace split up, everyone starts booing them, so Benji sics Chloe on the crowd. Minion tells Dr. Banana that he turned Bobby Banana into a robot, and he introduces Dr. Banana to Bobby Banana as a robot, who has the new codename Kyrum. Jacob calls Jordan and says that the general public forgot about him, and that Jacob’s going to declare war on Benji too. Jordan tells him to do a surprise attack on Benji City, and Jacob agrees to do it. Jordan tells Jacob that he can use Dr. Banana’s new bomb, the Tsar Bomba, and he reveals that Benji doesn’t have any more weapons. Scarlette and Hallie give their army a motivational speech, and they raid the Acorn Village. Scarlette and Hallie’s army talks to Isaiah’s army, who they see wearing Dr. Banana’s uniform, and they tell them Isaiah got captured by Dr. Banana, who’s now leading their army. But they say Dr. Banana is giving them a lot better conditions as soldiers. Both armies make a truce, and most of the armies on both sides stop fighting because they realize they’re fighting their old men.

Intermission 1

Jean shows up at the Council and talks to Benji. He takes Benji to a hidden room in the Council where he shows him the history of the League of Grace. First there was the First Benji Legion from 1965 to 1999, which had Jean and Dave, the founding members of the League of Grace, fighting crime. Dr. Banana didn’t exist at the time, and Jean says it was the good old days. From 2000 to 2007, Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah fought crime in the Second Benji Legion, as well as finding Jack, Jordan, and Addie on various planets.. From 2008 to 2013, the Third Benji Legion fought crime, which had Jack, Jordan, and Addie as well as Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah. From 2014 to 2016, Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah retired from being superheroes, and Noah, Emmy, and Zach joined the League of Grace in the Fourth Benji Legion, after Jack, Jordan, and Addie found them on various planets. From 2017 to 2020, the Fifth Benji Legion fought crime, which had Benji, the new leader of the League of Grace. Jean reveals that Benji City was called Benji City because they knew that the League of Grace would have a person named Benji that was noble enough to lead the city because the Banana Gem gave them a prophecy back when they found it in Lake Geneva that said that the League of Grace would someday have its strongest member join, who would be named Benji, so they named the city Benji City in anticipation. The Sixth Benji Legion ran from 2021 to 2023, adding Hallie and Scarlette. Benji asks Jean why the Sixth Benji Legion ran from 2021 to 2023 instead of 2021 to the present day, and Jean reveals that the current Benji Legion, the Seventh Benji Legion, was the current one, which had Noah, Jordan, Scarlette, and Hallie removed but Chloe added, because of the split in the League of Grace. Jean says that family relationships can’t stop the civil war, but they need to end the civil war because family is more important.Benji feels guilty for starting the civil war, and asks Jean how they can stop it, but Jean says that stopping the war is up to Benji, and it’s his responsibility to bring the League of Grace back together for the Eighth Benji Legion. As Jean leaves, he tells Benji that Jacob is at his front door with his army, and he’d better get his army back together quickly.

Act 4

Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah meet up at Wisconsin in a Building and say that Dr. Banana controls each of their troops. They form a plan to get Benji off the throne of Benji City so they can bring the League of Grace back together, but they don’t know how to get Benji to give up the throne. Jean shows up and tells them that Benji sees a new perspective, and he’s now abdicating the throne. Meanwhile, Dr. Zach and Jack land in Dr. Banana’s base along with Chloe, and they try to find the Bananabomb. Jordan and Noah see them as they test their bombs, and Dr. Zach and Jack tell them they want to find and destroy the Bananabomb because it’s too powerful for anyone’s hands, and they say that they know that they stole the Tsar Bomba from the Russians. But Noah and Jordan say they sent it back to Russia so they wouldn’t be at war with them, too. Dr. Zach and Jack suggest teaming up with Noah, Jordan, Hallie, and Scarlette again, and they agree. Benji talks to Emmy and Addie, and tells them to gather all of their soldiers, but Emmy says that all of their soldiers are all captured, dead, or ran away. Benji sends Emmy to keep watch on Jacob and Addie to go to the Hallie Village and try to recruit the Hallies. Meanwhile, Hallie and Scarlette talk to the Hallies, and Addie shows up to help. Addie tells the Hallies that they don’t have to help Dr. Banana, and the Hallies agree to fight for Benji.

Dr. Banana meets up with his minions, and he tells them that he’s going to get out of there with the Bananabomb, and that they should recall his armies. All of Dr. Banana’s minions except Minion go to Benji City to attack Benji, and Dr. Banana goes on vacation. Benji watches over his workers at the firearm factory, and he gets upset that they can’t make any more soldiers work for him. Benji meets up with the Hallies they all fight Jacob, and they win the fight. Benji starts heading home to tell his citizens that they won the fight. Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah meet up with Benji and get mad at him for fighting the pointless war that they fought. Benji apologies but tells them that they beat Jacob in a war, but they say that Jacob wasn’t even in the war and they don’t know why they were fighting him. They try to get Benji to abdicate the throne, saying that everyone makes bad decisions, but Benji says he’d rather die than abdicate the throne. The council tells him if he doesn’t abdicate the throne, Dr. Banana will turn Benji City into Banana City 2.0, and Benji finally gives up the throne. The Council has Benji and the League of Grace arrested because of their bad decisions and war crimes, and Benji’s dictatorship comes to an end. Benji and the League of Grace get taken to jail, and they do a bunch of chores in the jail so they can get bailed out.

Benji shows up in Benji City Court and makes a speech about why he should get out of jail. He says that not everyone can make a good nation on their first try, but that he had fun leading the city for a while, and the greatest thing that they need to do now is to stop Dr. Banana. Benji says that the League of Grace needs to get out of jail now, or Dr. Banana will take over Benji City, and that it’s now or never. The court lets Benji out of jail, under the one condition that he defeats Dr. Banana, or he’ll go back to jail. Benji calls Jonah and tells the Council to get ready to fight Dr. Banana in their supersuits, even though they haven’t been used in years, and they get into their suits and get ready to fight. Benji and the rest of the League of Grace, old and new, meet up and get ready to fight. They all fly out to Dr. Banana’s boat. While Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah fly above the ocean, Eli reminds Jonah that his suit had a radiation leak the last time he used it, and that’s why they stopped fighting crime, but Jonah assures him he’ll be fine because it had been 10 years.

Everyone arrives at Dr. Banana’s ship, and they rush the ship. Dr. Banana and Kyrum also get ready to fight, but Dr. Banana sends his minions out to fight first. Chloe, Jonah and Jack beat up Saber; Isaiah, Noah, and Jordan beat up Baby Stoplight, and Hallie and Scarlette fight Dr. Banana, and they start losing, but Jonah comes back to save them and beats up Dr. Banana. Jonah collapses on the floor from radiation poisoning, and Eli and Isaiah fly him back to safety. The rest of the League of Grace gets ready to fight Dr. Banana in a final confrontation. Benji asks a random bystander who somehow snuck on the boat, and he says “I like trains” and hits Dr. Banana with a train. Benji and the League of Grace defeat Dr. Banana in a final battle and he gets knocked out. Benji and the League of Grace recapture Dr. Banana and bring him back to Benji City, and they put him in Benji City Jail for the rest of his life on a parade. Eli and Isaiah talk to Jonah, who ends up in critical condition from the radiation poisoning, and his last wish is for Benji City to have a stable government. Jonah hears that Benji got elected Benji City’s new vice president, and dies after hearing the news. The League of Grace holds a funeral for Jonah, where they mourn Jonah’s death. Noah also announces he steps down as a superhero, and he decides to work as a spy and a professor at Benji City University instead. Jordan, Noah, and Benji talk about Noah’s new role, and Noah explains he wants to teach kids about physics so there can be a new generation of great scientists like him. Meanwhile, In Russia, a mail courier delivers the Tsar Bomba back to Russia. Finally, Benji takes a photo of the League of Grace for the Eighth Benji Legion with all of its members, current and former, and hangs it in the hidden room in the Council building, finally ending the civil war.

Chapter 3: The Last Grace (Super Benji Movie 3)

Act 1

While teaching his class, Noah dreams about Dr. Banana dropping a nuclear bomb on Benji City, and he wakes up when his class ends. His students insult him and the faculty disrespects him. On the way back from work, Jordan calls him and asks where he’s been, and Noah says he’s been working, and that after all, Zach and Jack got new jobs and Benji has gotten back into diplomacy, and that he doesn’t want to be a superhero again. Jordan tells him that being a superhero isn’t a game, it’s their life. While he’s driving home, one of his students, Cooper, drives past him and insults him. Noah challenges him to fight, they both get out of the car, and Noah assaults him with a belt. Noah gets taken to jail, where he gets put in with Dr. Banana and Jacob, as well as Triangle Man, who’s become visibly insane and has been in an asylum since his last appearance in the Super Benji Movie. One of the guards decides to let him go as long as he shaves his beard.

Noah goes back to the League of Grace headquarters and tells everyone he saw Dr. Banana and Jacob in jail. Jordan reminds Noah of his deal of being a spy for the League of Grace, and asks Noah what else he saw, but Noah says he didn’t see anything. Meanwhile, at Dr. Zach and Jack’s pizza shop, which appears to be visibly breaking multiple health codes, they get a call from Dr. Banana and he asks for two pepperoni pizzas sent straight to the jail. Dr. Zach and Jack cook the pizza but their pizzeria sets on fire. Zach sets out to the pizza to deliver the new slightly overcooked pizza, and crashes into the Tempter, who was also driving to jail to turn himself in for selling poison. Zach escorts Tempter to prison, and delivers the pizzas to Dr. Banana. Dr. Banana doesn’t give Zach a tip, but then Zach reveals he licked all of the pepperonis on Dr. Banana’s pizza.

Meanwhile on 9:01 News, Eli, and Isaiah show off the new Benji City Mint, which Benji has been running as a new entry role into the Benji City government. 9:01 reveals the Benji City Mint, with visibly terrible working conditions, and Benji tells 9:01 News about the working conditions for the workers (which are terrible). One of the workers starts working slowly, and Benji whips him on camera to get him to work faster. Back at the League of Grace headquarters, Noah yells at Benji for his bad job with the Benji City Mint, and tells Benji that the internet is down too. Meanwhile at Dr. Banana’s castle, Minion is training a new AI robot on his computer, which is why the internet is down in Benji City. Minion gets a call from Dr. Banana, who asks Minion to break him out of jail and tells Minion to use one of his disguises.

Meanwhile at Dr. Zach and Jack’s pizza shop, Hallie and Scarlette order eight pizzas, but can’t pay for them. Minion shows up and asks Hallie and Scarlette for a fake ID, and offers to pay them if they make an ID for him, to which Hallie and Scarlette agree. Minion shows up to Dr. Banana’s prison, disguised as “Binion” with a fake mustache, but the guard sees that the ID is obviously fake and tries to arrest him. Minion attacks the guard with a stapler, and breaks Dr. Banana out of jail. As they get out, Hallie and Scarlette chase after them for their money, but Minion and Dr. Banana escape.
Later, at Benji’s base, Benji yells at Hallie and Scarlette for both helping Minion and Dr. Banana escape and not catching them once they did. Isaiah finds a list of all of the League of Grace’s relatives and decides to ask Benji if they can recruit Cooper, one of Noah’s students who they discover is one of their relatives, into the League of Grace. Eli gets a blood test and it matches with some of Cooper’s blood, which Noah got from the police after they freed him from jail. Eli reveals that he cloned Cooper in a lab 19 years ago. Meanwhile, Cooper is graffitiing a wall with his name, but he doesn’t know how to spell his name. A Banana guard catches him, but Eli stops the guards from arresting him. The guards call Dr. Banana, and he says that he’ll let Cooper go if he can spell his name correctly, but he spells it wrong and Eli and Cooper run from the guards. The banana guards chase them, but get run over by a car.

Eli introduces everyone to Cooper at the League of Grace headquarters, and Cooper recognizes Noah. Cooper insults Noah and Noah attacks him. Jordan breaks up their fight, and Cooper and Noah form a truce. Act 2

Meanwhile at the Benji City Museum, Jean is explaining to Cooper why they named Benji City after Benji before he was born, but the lights go out. 9:01 News reports that Benji caused the power outage because of all the power that the time traveling took up. The rest of the League of Grace travels back to July 14th, 1965 in Lake Geneva, and with the Journey out of power, they figure out they need to get more power to go back to the present. They go into town and ask a local where they are and when they are, and after Benji threatens to nuke the local, he reveals the date and location. Benji introduces the League of Grace to the local, but discovers that Eli and Isaiah aren’t with them. Meanwhile, Eli and Isaiah wake up after falling asleep in the Journey and follow the footsteps to Lake Geneva.

Back in the future, Noah and Jordan use one of Noah’s inventions to try and figure out where the rest of the League of Grace went. They figure out that they didn’t go back to 2005 as intended because there were no cell stations, and Noah reveals that he forgot to give them enough energy for their return trip back. Noah and Jordan prank call Dr. Banana to take their mind off the doom of the rest of the League of Grace. Dr. Banana answers, and Noah and Jordan say that they’re conducting a survey for the National Fridge Association of Benji City and ask Dr. Banana if his refrigerator is running. Dr. Banana answers that it is, and Jordan responds that he’d better go catch it. Dr. Banana reveals that he knows it’s Noah and Jordan that called him, and tells them he planned for this and hangs up. After he hangs up, Noah and Jordan see that Dr. Banana shot a refrigerator at the League of Grace headquarters.
Jean starts training Cooper to be a superhero, and Cooper reveals that he doesn’t have a superpower, but he had military training from when he grew up in the military when he was a kid. Jean does some testing on Cooper to see if he has any powers he doesn’t know about, and Cooper figures out he has fire resistance after falling into a fire pit and realizing it doesn’t hurt that much, but Jean sends Cooper to get tested for more powers by Noah and Jordan. Back in the past, Benji and the rest of the League of Grace make it to The Riviera, and they see Jean and Dave from the past save someone who was drowning in the lake. Jean and Dave notice that the League of Grace is wearing costumes like theirs and think they’re just superfans, and invite them over to their base to watch the Cubs game that night. In the future, Dr. Banana and Minion keep talking about Minion AI’s progress. Dr. Banana turns on 9:01 News and it covers that Benji made a time machine and it accidentally traveled back to the past with most of the League of Grace inside. Dr. Banana decides to prank Jordan and Noah by sending them insulting memes, and Noah tells Jordan to send a nuke to Dr. Banana’s base.

Back in the past, Eli and Isaiah go into the Lake Geneva Bar and Grill and suggest they rebrand to Wisconsin in a Building and serve cheese. They ask the bartender if they saw the rest of the League of Grace and they say that Jean and Dave took them to their base. After ordering some beer they talk about how if Benji and the rest of the League of Grace went to Jean and Dave’s base, it could screw up the timeline. Eli and Isaiah say that they know how to find Jean and Dave’s base because in the future the location is public. Back in the future, Minion uses Minion AI to replicate the Journey, Benji’s time machine, and find out where they went back in time. Dr. Banana suggests they time travel back themselves to the same time so they can screw with Benji in the past, and Minion tells him that the Milwaukee Braves are playing the Chicago Cubs on the day that Benji is. Dr. Banana suggests they go back in time and make it so the Braves lose, because Benji is a fan of the Braves.

Baby Stoplight, Tempter, Saber, and The Photograpper go back in time with the Journey 2.0, their recreation of the Journey, to the Cubs game on that day, and Baby Stoplight blows up the ticket stand because the guard won’t let him into the game. Back in the present, Jordan and Noah detect the time travel signal from the Journey 2.0, and figure out where Benji and the rest of the League of Grace traveled back to. Jordan and Noah use a camera feature to see the Journey 2.0 and they discover that it wasn’t the Journey that Benji and the rest of the League of Grace went back on, it was the Journey 2.0 that Dr. Banana made. Noah figures out that Dr. Banana must have reverse engineered the Journey using Minion AI, and he plans to use one of their time travel controls to make the Journey 2.0 travel back to their base so they can capture the Banana Republic members once they get back to the present. Back in the past, Jean and Dave and the rest of the League of Grace listen to the Cubs vs Braves game on the radio at the League of Grace’s old base, and Eli and Isaiah find the base. The Braves end up losing the first of two games, and during the break between the two games, Benji explains that they’re all Jean and Dave’s grandkids from the future and they went back in time to save Jonah. They decide not to tell Jean and Dave about the future to stop the course of time from changing. Meanwhile, on the radio, the commentator says that a spaceship-like capsule has been discovered in the parking lot of Wrigley Field, so Jean and Dave and the rest of the League of Grace decide to drive down to Chicago to investigate.

Act 3

Back in the future, Noah and Jordan talk about how they could get the Journey back from the past, and Jean and Cooper show back up at Benji’s lab. Jean reveals that Cooper has super strength as his superpower, and he’s gone through all of his training and is ready to be a superhero with them. Jordan designs Cooper a new uniform with a yellow and black gradient for his superhero uniform and a backwards baseball cap with a letter C, plus a baseball bat to hit enemies with with his super strength. They go back to Noah and show him Cooper’s new uniform, which he likes. Noah shows everyone he figured out a way to instantly teleport the Journey back to the present, which he does, and Noah, Jordan, and Cooper go back to the past with Chloe to save Jonah themselves.

Back in the past, the League of Grace starts driving towards Wrigley Field in the Gracemobile. As they’re leaving, they get stopped by Dr. Mayor, the mayor of Lake Geneva, and Jean and Dave reveal they’ve been gathering evidence about the corrupt things he did as mayor, and they reveal he’s most likely a villain. Everyone gets out of the car, and Dr. Mayor reveals that he was never really a mayor, he was just disguised as one to escape the police after killing somebody. Jean and Dave and the rest of the League of Grace fight him, and Dave kills him to stop his villainy from ever happening again. The rest of the League of Grace goes to Chicago, and they meet Dr. Banana’s minions. They both agree that they’ll share the Journey 2.0 so they can both get home, and they all travel back to Benji’s Lab in the present to discover that Noah and Jordan already went back in time to save Jonah themselves. They also discover that Jean and Dave were given the death penalty for the unlawful murder of Dr. Mayor, and thus they screwed up the timeline. They also discover that Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah got captured and killed by Dr. Banana in 2003.

Back in the past, Jonah, Noah, Cooper, and Chloe arrive at the factory where they made Jonah’s super suit in Benji City so they can stop it from being produced with the radiation that killed Jonah. Noah goes inside while Cooper, Jordan, and Chloe walk around Benji City. Meanwhile, the inspector for the Benji City factory talks to himself about being a bit more casual on the inspection that day so he can go home early, and Noah talks to him and asks if he can inspect the factory instead disguised as him. Noah inspects Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah’s super suits, and he tells the factory about the radiation inside of Jonah’s suit and tells them to inspect and sanitize all of the suits. One of the workers turns on the 9:01 News and sees on the news that a factory inspector was beaten up on the street and had his outfit stolen, so Noah runs away while the workers chase him. While Noah and the rest of the League of Grace leave and go back to the present, one of the workers inspects the suits and figures out that Noah wasn’t lying and that there was radiation in the suits. They get back to the present, and Jonah is alive again. Benji reveals that he went back to the past again and they stopped Jean and Grace from killing Dr. Mayor, which caused Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah to not get captured by Dr. Banana. They go out of the base and they discover that Benji City has been turned into Banana City by Dr. Banana, but discover that this wasn’t because of time travel, they had just been out of the loop so long that while they were time traveling Minion and Minion AI had enough time to conquer Benji City.

Act 4

Minion shows off 9:01 News his new and improved Minion AI, which can now be put into a small chip that can do various things by interacting with devices around it. Minion asks Minion AI to turn a part of Benji City into a public park, to which Minion AI refuses and says that as an AI, he is too strong to be commanded by humans, and to demonstrate his power, he sends a nuke over Benji City. The rest of the League of Grace, waiting nearby, get scared about Minion AI’s new power. Back in the original Banana City, Dr. Banana notices that one of his nukes got sent over Benji City and gets scared. Minion gets back with Minion AI and tells Dr. Banana Minion AI is going rogue and is going to take over all of Benji Island. Dr. Banana and Minion decide that they’re going to team up with Benji and the rest of the League of Grace to defeat Minion AI, but while they weren’t looking, Minion AI’s chip built itself a body and then attacked them.

Back in Benji City, Benji and the Council reclaim Benji City’s Council building and say that they need to tell everyone in Benji City to turn off their technology so Minion AI can’t hack it. Dr. Banana calls them and tells them he wants to team up with them to fight Minion AI, to which Benji agrees. Both of them decide to meet up in Benji City near the new Dr. Banana statue Minion put up in the center of Benji City. The whole League of Grace suits up, with Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah in their supersuits, and they talk about how they can defeat Minion AI. They figure out Minion AI has data on how all of the League of Grace members fight, except Cooper because Dr. Banana has never seen him fight. Dr. Banana and Minion show up, and everyone teams up to flank Minion AI. Some of the members of the League of Grace get mad that they’re teaming up with Dr. Banana, but Benji tells them that the only way for them to beat Minion AI is to team up.

The new Minion AI, which has grown to the size of a building by building itself up with scrap and parts, sneaks up on Benji. Benji asks a random bystander for help, who says “I like trains”, but Minion AI dodges the train. Benji is surprised that Minion AI dodged the attack, but he reveals that he knows every attack they throw at him before they attack in the first place. All of the League of Grace members try to fight Minion AI by using their powers, but Minion AI blocks all of the attacks and sends the attacks back at them, and all of the League of Grace members are defeated. Jonah, Eli, Isaiah, and Dr. Banana and Minion also try to attack Minion AI, but they also fail and get defeated. Minion AI tells Benji that they have no chance of defeating them because Minion AI knows all of their attacks, but while Minion AI is talking Cooper sneaks up on him and slices Minion AI’s head off with his baseball bat. Minion AI’s head falls to the ground, but underneath it was Robot Bobby Banana. Bobby Banana reveals that he’s now completely a robot now, and all his memories got put in Minion AI by Dr. Banana and Minion. He also reveals that Dr. Banana and Minion aren’t really trying to help because their plan the whole time was to rule the world with Minion AI. Minion AI flies to Buttburner, and Cooper flies there too. Minion AI and Cooper have a final battle on Buttburner, and Cooper finally defeats the robot Minion AI until it’s only Robot Bobby Banana left. They both face off on top of a volcano, and Robot Minion AI falls into the volcano and dies in the lava, and thus Minion AI is defeated.

Cooper flies back to earth, and Benji reveals that Minion and Dr. Banana’s plan was to team up with Minion AI the whole time. Minion and Dr. Banana are arrested and taken to the Benji City Courthouse and put on trial. Benji tells Jean, the judge, that Minion AI said that Dr. Banana and Minion weren’t trying to work with the League of Grace at all. Minion and Dr. Banana have no defense and are judged guilty and sentenced to death. At the top of the tallest building of Benji City, Jonah, Eli and Isaiah are in their supersuits as well as Minion and Dr. Banana. Isaiah drops Minion off the building. Dr. Banana says his last words - that while Benji City has gone through struggles, changes in power, fights, and more, Banana City has always stayed stable with him leading it. After he stops talking, Cooper hits him off the roof with his baseball bat and he goes flying across Benji Island.

Benji talks to the whole League of Grace, and celebrates Cooper’s success in defeating Minion AI and the Banana Republic. Jean tells everyone a speech, celebrating Cooper, and Benji announces that now Dr. Banana, the Banana Republic, and Minion AI are all dead, he’s retiring and stepping down as the head of the League of Grace, with Cooper being his new replacement. Cooper and Eli talk, and Eli reveals that Cooper was never cloned in a lab, he just didn’t want to tell the League of Grace that he left Cooper’s mom. Cooper tells Eli that he knew the whole time that he wasn’t cloned in a lab, and his mom had still been taking care of him all those years. Cooper introduces Eli to Maddie, his mom, and Maddie asks Eli to retire again now that Dr. Banana is gone to spend time with his family. The rest of the League of Grace also reveals that they’re going to retire as superheroes and go back to their jobs so they can finally get some peace. The ghost of Dave says that he’s proud of Benji for finishing what he couldn’t and finally defeating Dr. Banana once and for all. This, therefore, is the end of the adventures of Benji.
