Super Benji Story

This official plot summary follows the canon Super Benji adventures in chronological order. Start with the Grace prequel, continue through the Adventures of Benji trilogy, then read Cooper in Beyond Benji. Struck-through passages on chapter pages identify material marked as non-canon in the source summary.
