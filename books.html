Read Super Benji Books and Comics

Super Benji Adventures

Super Benji Adventures is a webcomic series that follows the story of the Super Benji Movies and retells them in a new format, with new art, new stories, new lore, and much more. The webcomic is currently being released, with new pages being released frequently.

Super Benji Rewritten

Read Book 1

Super Benji Rewritten is a series of two books that follow the plot of the first and second Super Benji movies. The first book is a Choose your Own Adventure-styled book that follows Benji as he recovers the Banana Gem from Dr. Banana. The second book is a book recreation of the second Super Benji Movie.

The second book is incomplete, but you can find the first book here.

The Minion Manifesto

Read Now

The Minion Manifesto is a Super Benji book told from the perspective of Minion AI. It covers key events from the Super Benji series, including the plot of Super Benji Movie 3. Read it online for free and gain deeper insights into Minion AI’s thoughts and motivations. Some of the lore in this book is outdated though as it was released along with the Super Benji Movie 3.
