Have you ever wanted to make your own Super Benji comics, animations, fangames, etc. and have them featured in Super Benji Adventures? The Super Benji Adventures Fanwork Contest is your time to shine! Contest runs until January 1st.

The event runs from now until January 1st. Create your Super Benji fanwork and submit it in the Super Benji Discord server or on X. The top submissions will be featured in Super Benji Adventures.

Rules

Submission Eligibility

  • Anyone can participate and submit entries.
  • You can submit multiple entries.
  • Collaborative submissions are allowed.
  • Any types of fanwork is eligible as long as it can be uploaded online for submission.
  • Submissions must be original fanworks based on the Super Benji series.
  • You may use Super Benji locations, characters, settings, and other assets.

Submission Guidelines

  • Plagiarism, stolen art, and submissions that are created by other people are prohibited.
  • Submissions must be appropriate for a general audience. No NSFW or 18+ content.
  • No AI-generated submissions are allowed, with the exception of AI use in programming.

Submission Requirements

  • Submissions must be through the official Super Benji Discord server or on X.
  • Use the hashtag #SuperBenjiFanworkContest on X. Tagging @SuperBenjiShow is recommended.
  • You can join the Super Benji Discord server at https://discord.gg/WJzbMhtgDA.
  • Submit your work with all collaborators credited.
  • Submissions must be received before January 1st.

Judging and Showcasing

  • All valid entries may be showcased or reposted on Super Benji platforms.
  • Selected submissions may be featured in Super Benji Adventures.
  • Creators will receive credit when their fanwork is featured.
  • Creators retain ownership and copyright of their original contributions.
  • By entering, participants grant the Super Benji team permission to display, repost, and showcase their submissions for contest-related promotional purposes.
