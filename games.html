Play Super Benji Games

Super Benji has appeared in several video games, featuring stories that take place before, during, and after the Super Benji Show.

Super Benji Worlds

Super Benji Worlds is an upcoming platformer by FC Studios. The game plays through the story of the Super Benji Movie. In this game, you venture through Benji Island and defeat the minions of Dr. Banana, before eventually defeating Dr. Banana himself as the final boss.

Benji's Nuclear Weapons Testing

Benji's Nuclear Weapons Testing is a satirical and explosive game where players upgrade Benji’s nuclear arsenal. Improve your weapons, advance scientific research, and unlock multiple endings. Target famous, infamous, and hidden locations from across the Super Benji universe. Available now on Itch.io!


Super Benji Trading Card Game

Super Benji TCG (Trading Card Game) is the official card game tie-in to the Super Benji Show and Movies. Battle for control of Benji Island by building your deck and deploying characters from the League of Grace or Banana Republic. Play Characters to clash on the battlefield, then shift momentum with clever Actions, game-changing Locations, and powerful Artifacts.

The objective is simple: Defeat your opponent by lowering their health before they lower yours.
