Chapter 1: Cooper

Act 1

In a flashback, Cooper walks into a sporting goods store to buy a baseball bat. After selling it to him, the shopkeeper, Joe, asks Cooper why he needs a baseball bat, and he says that he wants one because he sees a lot of robberies around town and wonders if he could be next.

Joe reassures him that Benji City is a lot safer than the suburbs, because of the League of Grace fighting crime, and that Benji City is in good hands, to which Cooper replies that he sure hopes so.

Back in the present, after the events of the Super Benji Movie 3, Cooper is sitting on his couch watching the 9:01 News. 9:01 News reveals that Minion, Dr. Banana’s second in command, might not be dead like everyone thinks.

The Benji City Bank was robbed the night before while the guards were asleep, and Dr. Banana’s trademarked weapons were found on the scene. Since Dr. Banana doesn’t let anyone else use his weapons and Benji City’s collection of Dr. Banana’s confiscated weapons hadn’t been stolen, 9:01 News concludes it must have been the work of Minion.

Cooper, watching the news, remarks that the city has been really crime-ridden since Benji stepped down, and he decides to stop watching TV and start patrolling Benji City for crime.

He goes to the bank and starts looking for crime, and a bank robber drives right past him with bags of money, so Cooper runs after him and jumps in a car. Cooper chases the robber for a bit until the robber stops at a green light and reveals that he’s actually colorblind, after which Cooper crashes into him and captures him.

In the Benji City Jail, Cooper talks to the robber, Rob Berr. Cooper asks if Minion was the one who hired him, and Rob says that Minion is gone from the criminal world, and he was hired by someone who looked like a banana.

Meanwhile, at the Benji City Public Library, a kid, Steven, is looking for games on the library computers when he finds a program called “My Super Cool AI” and runs it to see what it does.

The AI, who shows itself to be very glitchy, introduces itself to be the beta version of My Super Cool AI, and tries to say who it was programmed by but glitches out.

Meanwhile, Minion reveals to Dr. Banana, who is living in a cave, that some people are ready for him, to which Dr. Banana remarks that he’ll finally have the tools he needs to take over Benji City.
