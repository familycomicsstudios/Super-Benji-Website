The Adventures of Benji

Chapter 1: Super Benji Adventures (Super Benji Movie)

Act 1

Dr. Banana steals the Banana Gem from Benji, and brings it up to his secret base to use it to power a superweapon with the powers to destroy the world. Benji goes up to Dr. Banana’s secret base to try to get it back but Dr. Banana opens a trapdoor beneath him and he falls back to earth. He goes back to the League of Grace and tells them what happened. Dr. Banana delivers a poisoned pizza to the League of Grace and Hallie eats it and gets sick. Noah says that he put a tracking device on Dr. Banana. They have Dr. Zach and Dr. Jack help Hallie, and Benji, Jordan and Noah go to find Dr. Banana.

They go on their sleds to Benji City to go find Dr. Banana, but while they do so Jack calls them and says they found out the pizza was poisoned. They try to tell Benji what Dr. Banana’s plan was but the call cuts out. Tempter gets sent by Dr. Banana to fight Benji and crew but he loses. Scarlette joins them to help. Later, Dr. Banana shows Tempter the Bananabot 2.0 which is allegedly the same as the Bananabot 1.0. Benji and crew get to Benji City and they go to Wisconsin in a Building where Benji tries to order beer but he’s only 4 years old. Emmy comes to them and tells them that Addie got kidnapped by Dr. Banana.

Act 2 Part 1

Dr. Banana also kidnaps Addie and takes her to one of his secret bases. Addie is shown to be getting dangled over lava and Dr. Banana reveals that they’re trying to use Addie as bait to lure the League of Grace so that the Banana Republic can defeat them. Dr. Banana sets a 24 hour timer that lowers Addie into the lava. Emmy joins them and they keep going towards Dr. Banana. Jack tries to become Tinfoil Man by putting tinfoil on his head, then he gets struck by lightning and teleports for a bit to talk to Benji and crew.

Dr. Banana then sends Baby Stoplight to attack Benji and crew and Benji looks Baby Stoplight in his red light and gets zapped. They ask a random bystander for help and he says “I Like Trains” and Baby Stoplight and the I Like Trains Guy get hit by a train. Dr. Banana and Saber find out where Benji and crew are and send a nuclear bomb straight to their location. Benji and crew get hit by the nuclear bomb, and Dr. Banana and Saber see the blast radius and not Benji and crew. Benji and crew hid in a hole to survive the nuclear bomb. Zach also joins the party. Meanwhile, Dr. Banana sends Jack a bribe of 10 million dollars plus more to be a spy for him and tell him lots of information about Benji.

Act 2 Part 2

Benji and crew climb Chestnut Mountain. They go to the bottom of Chestnut Mountain and fight Bobby Banana, Dr. Banana’s son, to get past the Prison house, which Bobby Banana is guarding. They get into the prison house and then fight Tempter. Tempter beats Benji and crew but Scarlette beats up the Tempter after everybody else gets kicked out. They go to the Acorn Village and also into the Wisconsin in a Building in the village. The Photograpper, another one of Dr. Banana’s minions, tells the bartender that Benji doesn’t pay his taxes (which Benji confesses to by accident) and Benji gets sent to jail. Meanwhile Jack and Hallie come to bail out Benji. Meanwhile in Banana City Dr. Banana declares himself Emperor of the Universe.

Dr. Banana receives a letter from Benji and crew which contains Bobby Banana’s dead body. Benji and crew go across Grace Bay. Meanwhile Addie escapes. Tempter and Saber, who are guarding her, are wearing human masks to disguise themselves so Addie doesn’t attack them, but Addie attacks them and escapes. They go into Lake GenevaThe Grace Bay and fight Baby Stoplight again as well as his kids. They beat all of them up. Addie goes to Hallie Village, which is near Banana City, and asks where Benji and crew are. The crew then goes to The Crystal Caves.

Baby Stoplight invades Hallie Village, but Benji and crew show up and they fight Baby Stoplight again. Dr. Banana’s minion, Minion, tells Dr. Banana that Baby Stoplight lost twice and failed to stop them. Dr. Banana goes to Benji City. They have the Bananabot protect the castle too. Dr. Banana steals a bunch of nuclear bombs from Benji’s base. Scarlette also goes missing. Scarlette then comes back a bit later to tell them the Bananabot is coming back to beat up the crew. Dr. Banana pilots the Bananabot to beat everyone up. But Benji’s crew beats up Dr. Banana and the Bananabot. The Bananabot ends up in critical condition and it can’t fight anymore. Benji arrives in, and then burns down a bunch of stuff, in Banana City.

Act 2 Part 3

Dr. Banana sends out the Bananabot again, even though it was in critical condition. Benji declares war on Dr. Banana for stealing his nukes. They fight the Bananabot again, this time without Dr. Banana controlling it. Benji and the Bananabot insult each other. While they fight Dr. Banana sends one of Benji’s nukes towards Benji City. It lands in the Benji City Clearing and hits Jordan, who got hit all the way back to Benji City while they fought the Bananabot. People in Benji City riot to ban superheroes because they think it was Benji who nuked them due to the fact it was one of Benji’s nukes. Mayor Labelle finds out that it was actually Dr. Banana because he stole Benji’s nukes.

At Dr. Banana’s castle everyone fights Dr. Banana, and Jordan comes back to help. Dr. Banana gets put under arrest by one of Benji City’s soldiers. But for some reason Dr. Banana is reported dead, and everyone celebrates, including the Benji City Council, who try to charge Jordan for accidental manslaughter for killing Dr. Banana. Mayor Labelle parades through Benji City now that Dr. Banana is dead but they get nuked and assassinated by one of Dr. Banana’s minions. Meanwhile Benji and crew find out Dr. Banana’s body is missing. They find a letter from Dr. Banana that says he faked his death. They look at Dr. Banana’s search history to find where he went and discover he left for Buttburner.

Act 3

Benji and crew go on a spaceship heading straight for Buttburner. However it crash lands on the Lightning Planet. They fight The Photograpper there and he gets defeated. Dr. Banana makes it to Buttburner and hides away in his castle. They head to another rocket ship across the planet. Meanwhile on the ship Benji makes a band called BenjiBeatz. Meanwhile Triangle Man tries to cook a pizza on the rope where Addie was, and the timer ends and the pizza explodes. Triangle Man then dies. Benji’s ship crashes again on Cookie Island. For some reason they meet various Veggietales characters. But a Hallie lands on them and kills them.

Meanwhile, Dr. Banana tracks Benji’s current location and launches a nuke at them. They get back on the ship and make another BenjiBeatz song because the first one got #1 on the charts (but they got sued cause it was a cover song). They go #1 again, but get sued again for making their song the same as another one. They get to Buttburner but get shot out of the sky. A guy makes them tell a Yo Mama joke to enter Buttburner, and they manage to get in. They go to Buttburner City, which they discover has a lot of industrial businesses. Jacob, a shadowy figure, seemingly helps them get to Dr. Banana’s castle on Buttburner, but instead of helping he is revealed to be Dr. Banana’s spy, and throws everyone into a volcano.

Hallie turns big and everyone rides on her to survive the volcano as the lava floats up. They find a secret camera Dr. Banana placed. They head back up to Dr. Banana’s castle and Dr. Banana sends Jacob to attack them. Scarlette attacks Jacob and beats him up. They get to Dr. Banana’s castle. Benji flies into Dr. Banana’s spaceship as Benji and Dr. Banana have an epic battle. Benji steals the Banana Gem back and flies out of Dr. Banana’s spaceship. They have another epic battle with Dr. Banana and defeat him. Benji discovers that cantaloupes grant him flight.
Everyone gets back down to earth and Jean, the grandmother of the League of Grace and one of the founders of the League of Grace, congratulates them all for saving the world by returning the Banana Gem back to the League of Grace. BenjiBeatz makes another rap song to celebrate. They find out that Dr. Banana nuked Benji’s base as a last resort and it’s almost destroyed. Jonah calls the League of Grace to get them ready to train for another fight…
