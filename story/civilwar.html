Civil War

Chapter 2: Civil War (Super Benji Movie 2)

Act 1

Benji dreams about Dr. Banana’s troops invading Benji’s base. In the dream Dr. Banana and his army march into Benji’s base, but as they’re about to reach it Benji wakes up screaming. Benji goes outside and sees Dr. Banana actually outside of Benji’s base. Benji talks to Dr. Banana and Dr. Banana talks about a treaty to not attack Benji for now. Benji tells the League of Grace he needs to go to a Benji City meeting to restore their government after Mayor Labelle’s death, and Benji says he’s going to try and rule Benji City legally. Meanwhile, Jordan and Noah and Tempter and Saber are all gambling at a casino. Noah and Jordan try to cheat with mirror glasses but they realize that the glasses actually show Tempter and Saber their cards instead of letting them see theirs. Scarlette tries to help Noah and Jordan escape by threatening to shoot everyone in the casino. Tempter calls 911 and tells them that Scarlette is threatening to shoot everyone. The 911 operator figures out that Tempter is a Banana Republic member and says he doesn’t get paid enough to help and says Tempter and Saber can do whatever they want. Scarlette shoots Tempter and Saber and Noah and Jordan and Scarlette go home.

Back at League of Grace HQ, everyone says that if the treaty doesn’t work then Dr. Banana will attack Benji for sure. Scarlette suggests that if Dr. Banana doesn’t sign the treaty, they’ll all attack Dr. Banana, and everyone agrees. The League of Grace gets mailed a video from Jonah, their cousin and a Benji City diplomat, who says that they’ve trained a new member of the League of Grace, a dog named Chloe. Chloe starts talking to everyone by barking and everyone seems to understand her. Meanwhile, Benji meets with the Benji City Council, their three cousins Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah. Benji gets a call from Dr. Banana where he says that he’s declining the treaty, so Benji declares war on Dr. Banana on behalf of Benji City. Meanwhile, Jacob tries to assassinate Benji but can’t bring himself to fire. The council tells Benji that they can’t let him go to war against Dr. Banana because it’ll ruin Benji City’s uncorrupted politics as well as the economy, and they throw Benji out of the window. Benji gets fed up with Benji City’s alleged corrupt government, and starts planning to overthrow the government in a dictatorship that Benji runs.

The council throws a party now that Benji’s gone, but Jacob shoots Eli instead of Benji and Eli gets taken to the hospital. Now that Eli leaves, Benji goes into the Council building to try and overthrow it. Benji fights two guards to get back into the building, and then declares himself emperor of Benji City. He calls Scarlette, who asks him why he overthrew the government if both Benji and the government were both capitalist, and Benji responds by saying that he was upset the government wouldn’t let him go to war. Benji makes a public appearance as emperor. His first order of business is to declare war on Dr. Banana, and his second order of business is to remove freedom of speech and he declares severe punishment on anyone speaking negatively of him or positively of Dr. Banana. An audience member tells Benji he sucks and Benji arrests him. On 9:01 News they say that Benji overthrew the government and that Benji City is now a capitalist monarchy.

Act 2

Dr. Banana and Minion discuss that Benji is now the emperor, and that Benji plans to declare war on him. Dr. Banana discovers he’s vastly outnumbered, but Minion tells him a genius plan: to recruit League of Grace members to his side to balance the odds. Benji goes to Wisconsin in a Building and Baby Stoplight, disguised as a bartender, annoys Benji so much that he leaves. The League of Grace meets at the Benji City Council and they decide on new laws to implement. Emmy suggests new police officers, because Benji hasn’t been doing an amazing job of stopping crime. Scarlette suggests making Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah diplomats for Benji City. Benji calls Dr. Banana and tells him to sign the treaty or Benji will nuke him. Dr. Banana invites Benji to his castle to discuss the treaty, and Benji comes to meet with him. They discuss some terms, including that their militaries won’t fight each other and that they won’t nuke each other except in self defense. Both of them sign the treaty.

Dr. Banana and Benji make a public appearance in Benji City. Dr. Banana says that Benji’s been treating his citizens unfairly and says that his unfair treatment ends now that they’re in a treaty. While they talk a nuke flies above their head. Dr. Banana says it wasn’t one of his, and they start fighting. Meanwhile, Jacob says that Dr. Banana backstabbed him and Benji doesn’t like him, but Jordan comes to talk with him. Jacob asks Jordan to share his secrets, and Jordan asks for 3 million dollars and a supercar. Jacob takes the offer and Jordan reveals Benji is going to backstab Dr. Banana now that they have a treaty, as well as that he’s going to expand his empire all the way across Benji Island, and limit everyone’s free speech. Jonah and Isaiah meet Eli in the hospital, who is recovering quickly. In the hospital they watch 9:01 News, which reveals that Dr. Banana and Benji have sealed their alliance, but a nuclear bomb hit Benji City while they were talking. The news also says that the nuke wasn’t either Benji’s nor Dr. Banana’s.

Benji and Dr. Banana talk outside of the Council building, and Dr. Banana lets it slip that he wants to turn Benji City into a communist city. Benji and Dr. Banana kick the rest of the League of Grace out of the Council and they discuss laws to make, which include ones that say that Dr. Banana’s supporters get thrown in jail. Benji tries to ban the letter B because B is for Banana but he doesn’t realize that Benji starts with B as well. Jacob and Minion, at Dr. Banana’s castle, fire another nuke at Benji City, and it hits Eli as he walks out of the hospital. Benji and Noah test their new nuclear bomb on Banana City but they fire the wrong bomb, and the one they fired was a confetti one. Dr. Banana sneaks into Benji’s base to try and find the nuclear launch codes, but Chloe stops him. 9:01 News reveals that Dr. Banana and Benji have both been breaking their treaty, because Dr. Banana snuck into Benji’s base, and because Benji launched a nuke at Banana City. Benji tells Dr. Banana that their alliance probably won’t work, so they break up the alliance under one condition. Dr. Banana and Benji talk to the rest of the League of Grace, and Dr. Banana says that anyone who wants to stay on Benji’s side can go to the left side of the room, and anyone who wants to join him can go to the right side. But Dr. Banana turns off the lights in the room, and Dr. Banana tricks everyone into having Jordan, Noah, Scarlette, and Hallie join his team because they accidentally walked to his side.

Act 3

Dr. Banana and his new allies go back to his base, and Benji and the rest of the League of Grace - Addie, Emmy, Zach, and Jack - go back to the Council. Benji says that each member of his team, including the Council, gets to train their own army to help fight Dr. Banana. Dr. Banana talks with Noah and Jordan, and they talk to him about new ideas for bombs Dr. Banana can make. They show him the Bananabomb, which would recycle the Bananabot and turn it into a new bomb. Dr. Banana says that he doesn’t want them to recycle the Bananabot, but Noah and Jordan tell Dr. Banana that they already made the bomb. Dr. Banana wants to use it immediately to nuke Benji City, but Noah and Jordan tell him it’ll destroy the whole world if they use it. Minion tells Scarlette and Hallie that the Banana Republic is broke, and it’s because Scarlette and Hallie ate a lot of cheese curds with the Bananas’ credit card. Scarlette and Hallie tell Minion they convinced Eli and his army to switch to Dr. Banana’s side.

9:01 News says that Dr. Banana sends Eli’s army to attack Benji City first. Zach and Jonah’s army fights one of Dr. Banana’s armies, and Zach tells Jonah about the Bananabomb. Jonah tells Zach that him and Jack should turn into Dr. Zach and Jack and go look for the Bananabomb so they can find out how to recreate it, but Zach reveals that Noah and Jordan were the ones who made it. Dr. Banana’s army uses fart bombs against Isaiah’s army, and he calls Benji to ask for help. Benji tells him that he has the advantage and he should keep fighting, but Isaiah says that his army sucks and doesn’t know how to fight. So Benji starts a draft in Benji City so they can get more good soldiers. Isaiah disagrees, so Benji says that he’s on his own. Jonah and Isaiah coordinate to do an assault on Dr. Banana’s army as a diversion, but then use tanks once Dr. Banana’s army is distracted. They start doing the plan, and Dr. Zach and Jack use a tank to attack, but it gets stuck in the mud. They see Eli, who joined Dr. Banana’s side, and Eli reveals he’s getting paid more by Dr. Banana. Dr. Zach and Jack fight Eli, but he doesn’t have powers, so he uses his supersuit to get the upper hand in the fight. But his suit hadn’t been used since 2009 so it takes a while to start up. Dr. Zach and Jack fight Eli in his supersuit. While Eli fights, he accidentally throws Zach and Jack into Dr. Banana’s castle.

Meanwhile, Benji and Emmy campaign in Benji City, but when Benji says that the League of Grace split up, everyone starts booing them, so Benji sics Chloe on the crowd. Minion tells Dr. Banana that he turned Bobby Banana into a robot, and he introduces Dr. Banana to Bobby Banana as a robot, who has the new codename Kyrum. Jacob calls Jordan and says that the general public forgot about him, and that Jacob’s going to declare war on Benji too. Jordan tells him to do a surprise attack on Benji City, and Jacob agrees to do it. Jordan tells Jacob that he can use Dr. Banana’s new bomb, the Tsar Bomba, and he reveals that Benji doesn’t have any more weapons. Scarlette and Hallie give their army a motivational speech, and they raid the Acorn Village. Scarlette and Hallie’s army talks to Isaiah’s army, who they see wearing Dr. Banana’s uniform, and they tell them Isaiah got captured by Dr. Banana, who’s now leading their army. But they say Dr. Banana is giving them a lot better conditions as soldiers. Both armies make a truce, and most of the armies on both sides stop fighting because they realize they’re fighting their old men.

Intermission 1

Jean shows up at the Council and talks to Benji. He takes Benji to a hidden room in the Council where he shows him the history of the League of Grace. First there was the First Benji Legion from 1965 to 1999, which had Jean and Dave, the founding members of the League of Grace, fighting crime. Dr. Banana didn’t exist at the time, and Jean says it was the good old days. From 2000 to 2007, Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah fought crime in the Second Benji Legion, as well as finding Jack, Jordan, and Addie on various planets.. From 2008 to 2013, the Third Benji Legion fought crime, which had Jack, Jordan, and Addie as well as Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah. From 2014 to 2016, Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah retired from being superheroes, and Noah, Emmy, and Zach joined the League of Grace in the Fourth Benji Legion, after Jack, Jordan, and Addie found them on various planets. From 2017 to 2020, the Fifth Benji Legion fought crime, which had Benji, the new leader of the League of Grace. Jean reveals that Benji City was called Benji City because they knew that the League of Grace would have a person named Benji that was noble enough to lead the city because the Banana Gem gave them a prophecy back when they found it in Lake Geneva that said that the League of Grace would someday have its strongest member join, who would be named Benji, so they named the city Benji City in anticipation. The Sixth Benji Legion ran from 2021 to 2023, adding Hallie and Scarlette. Benji asks Jean why the Sixth Benji Legion ran from 2021 to 2023 instead of 2021 to the present day, and Jean reveals that the current Benji Legion, the Seventh Benji Legion, was the current one, which had Noah, Jordan, Scarlette, and Hallie removed but Chloe added, because of the split in the League of Grace. Jean says that family relationships can’t stop the civil war, but they need to end the civil war because family is more important.Benji feels guilty for starting the civil war, and asks Jean how they can stop it, but Jean says that stopping the war is up to Benji, and it’s his responsibility to bring the League of Grace back together for the Eighth Benji Legion. As Jean leaves, he tells Benji that Jacob is at his front door with his army, and he’d better get his army back together quickly.

Act 4

Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah meet up at Wisconsin in a Building and say that Dr. Banana controls each of their troops. They form a plan to get Benji off the throne of Benji City so they can bring the League of Grace back together, but they don’t know how to get Benji to give up the throne. Jean shows up and tells them that Benji sees a new perspective, and he’s now abdicating the throne. Meanwhile, Dr. Zach and Jack land in Dr. Banana’s base along with Chloe, and they try to find the Bananabomb. Jordan and Noah see them as they test their bombs, and Dr. Zach and Jack tell them they want to find and destroy the Bananabomb because it’s too powerful for anyone’s hands, and they say that they know that they stole the Tsar Bomba from the Russians. But Noah and Jordan say they sent it back to Russia so they wouldn’t be at war with them, too. Dr. Zach and Jack suggest teaming up with Noah, Jordan, Hallie, and Scarlette again, and they agree. Benji talks to Emmy and Addie, and tells them to gather all of their soldiers, but Emmy says that all of their soldiers are all captured, dead, or ran away. Benji sends Emmy to keep watch on Jacob and Addie to go to the Hallie Village and try to recruit the Hallies. Meanwhile, Hallie and Scarlette talk to the Hallies, and Addie shows up to help. Addie tells the Hallies that they don’t have to help Dr. Banana, and the Hallies agree to fight for Benji.

Dr. Banana meets up with his minions, and he tells them that he’s going to get out of there with the Bananabomb, and that they should recall his armies. All of Dr. Banana’s minions except Minion go to Benji City to attack Benji, and Dr. Banana goes on vacation. Benji watches over his workers at the firearm factory, and he gets upset that they can’t make any more soldiers work for him. Benji meets up with the Hallies they all fight Jacob, and they win the fight. Benji starts heading home to tell his citizens that they won the fight. Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah meet up with Benji and get mad at him for fighting the pointless war that they fought. Benji apologies but tells them that they beat Jacob in a war, but they say that Jacob wasn’t even in the war and they don’t know why they were fighting him. They try to get Benji to abdicate the throne, saying that everyone makes bad decisions, but Benji says he’d rather die than abdicate the throne. The council tells him if he doesn’t abdicate the throne, Dr. Banana will turn Benji City into Banana City 2.0, and Benji finally gives up the throne. The Council has Benji and the League of Grace arrested because of their bad decisions and war crimes, and Benji’s dictatorship comes to an end. Benji and the League of Grace get taken to jail, and they do a bunch of chores in the jail so they can get bailed out.

Benji shows up in Benji City Court and makes a speech about why he should get out of jail. He says that not everyone can make a good nation on their first try, but that he had fun leading the city for a while, and the greatest thing that they need to do now is to stop Dr. Banana. Benji says that the League of Grace needs to get out of jail now, or Dr. Banana will take over Benji City, and that it’s now or never. The court lets Benji out of jail, under the one condition that he defeats Dr. Banana, or he’ll go back to jail. Benji calls Jonah and tells the Council to get ready to fight Dr. Banana in their supersuits, even though they haven’t been used in years, and they get into their suits and get ready to fight. Benji and the rest of the League of Grace, old and new, meet up and get ready to fight. They all fly out to Dr. Banana’s boat. While Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah fly above the ocean, Eli reminds Jonah that his suit had a radiation leak the last time he used it, and that’s why they stopped fighting crime, but Jonah assures him he’ll be fine because it had been 10 years.

Everyone arrives at Dr. Banana’s ship, and they rush the ship. Dr. Banana and Kyrum also get ready to fight, but Dr. Banana sends his minions out to fight first. Chloe, Jonah and Jack beat up Saber; Isaiah, Noah, and Jordan beat up Baby Stoplight, and Hallie and Scarlette fight Dr. Banana, and they start losing, but Jonah comes back to save them and beats up Dr. Banana. Jonah collapses on the floor from radiation poisoning, and Eli and Isaiah fly him back to safety. The rest of the League of Grace gets ready to fight Dr. Banana in a final confrontation. Benji asks a random bystander who somehow snuck on the boat, and he says “I like trains” and hits Dr. Banana with a train. Benji and the League of Grace defeat Dr. Banana in a final battle and he gets knocked out. Benji and the League of Grace recapture Dr. Banana and bring him back to Benji City, and they put him in Benji City Jail for the rest of his life on a parade. Eli and Isaiah talk to Jonah, who ends up in critical condition from the radiation poisoning, and his last wish is for Benji City to have a stable government. Jonah hears that Benji got elected Benji City’s new vice president, and dies after hearing the news. The League of Grace holds a funeral for Jonah, where they mourn Jonah’s death. Noah also announces he steps down as a superhero, and he decides to work as a spy and a professor at Benji City University instead. Jordan, Noah, and Benji talk about Noah’s new role, and Noah explains he wants to teach kids about physics so there can be a new generation of great scientists like him. Meanwhile, In Russia, a mail courier delivers the Tsar Bomba back to Russia. Finally, Benji takes a photo of the League of Grace for the Eighth Benji Legion with all of its members, current and former, and hangs it in the hidden room in the Council building, finally ending the civil war.
