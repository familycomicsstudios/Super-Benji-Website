The Last Grace

Chapter 3: The Last Grace (Super Benji Movie 3)

Act 1

While teaching his class, Noah dreams about Dr. Banana dropping a nuclear bomb on Benji City, and he wakes up when his class ends. His students insult him and the faculty disrespects him. On the way back from work, Jordan calls him and asks where he’s been, and Noah says he’s been working, and that after all, Zach and Jack got new jobs and Benji has gotten back into diplomacy, and that he doesn’t want to be a superhero again. Jordan tells him that being a superhero isn’t a game, it’s their life. While he’s driving home, one of his students, Cooper, drives past him and insults him. Noah challenges him to fight, they both get out of the car, and Noah assaults him with a belt. Noah gets taken to jail, where he gets put in with Dr. Banana and Jacob, as well as Triangle Man, who’s become visibly insane and has been in an asylum since his last appearance in the Super Benji Movie. One of the guards decides to let him go as long as he shaves his beard.

Noah goes back to the League of Grace headquarters and tells everyone he saw Dr. Banana and Jacob in jail. Jordan reminds Noah of his deal of being a spy for the League of Grace, and asks Noah what else he saw, but Noah says he didn’t see anything. Meanwhile, at Dr. Zach and Jack’s pizza shop, which appears to be visibly breaking multiple health codes, they get a call from Dr. Banana and he asks for two pepperoni pizzas sent straight to the jail. Dr. Zach and Jack cook the pizza but their pizzeria sets on fire. Zach sets out to the pizza to deliver the new slightly overcooked pizza, and crashes into the Tempter, who was also driving to jail to turn himself in for selling poison. Zach escorts Tempter to prison, and delivers the pizzas to Dr. Banana. Dr. Banana doesn’t give Zach a tip, but then Zach reveals he licked all of the pepperonis on Dr. Banana’s pizza.

Meanwhile on 9:01 News, Eli, and Isaiah show off the new Benji City Mint, which Benji has been running as a new entry role into the Benji City government. 9:01 reveals the Benji City Mint, with visibly terrible working conditions, and Benji tells 9:01 News about the working conditions for the workers (which are terrible). One of the workers starts working slowly, and Benji whips him on camera to get him to work faster. Back at the League of Grace headquarters, Noah yells at Benji for his bad job with the Benji City Mint, and tells Benji that the internet is down too. Meanwhile at Dr. Banana’s castle, Minion is training a new AI robot on his computer, which is why the internet is down in Benji City. Minion gets a call from Dr. Banana, who asks Minion to break him out of jail and tells Minion to use one of his disguises.

Meanwhile at Dr. Zach and Jack’s pizza shop, Hallie and Scarlette order eight pizzas, but can’t pay for them. Minion shows up and asks Hallie and Scarlette for a fake ID, and offers to pay them if they make an ID for him, to which Hallie and Scarlette agree. Minion shows up to Dr. Banana’s prison, disguised as “Binion” with a fake mustache, but the guard sees that the ID is obviously fake and tries to arrest him. Minion attacks the guard with a stapler, and breaks Dr. Banana out of jail. As they get out, Hallie and Scarlette chase after them for their money, but Minion and Dr. Banana escape.
Later, at Benji’s base, Benji yells at Hallie and Scarlette for both helping Minion and Dr. Banana escape and not catching them once they did. Isaiah finds a list of all of the League of Grace’s relatives and decides to ask Benji if they can recruit Cooper, one of Noah’s students who they discover is one of their relatives, into the League of Grace. Eli gets a blood test and it matches with some of Cooper’s blood, which Noah got from the police after they freed him from jail. Eli reveals that he cloned Cooper in a lab 19 years ago. Meanwhile, Cooper is graffitiing a wall with his name, but he doesn’t know how to spell his name. A Banana guard catches him, but Eli stops the guards from arresting him. The guards call Dr. Banana, and he says that he’ll let Cooper go if he can spell his name correctly, but he spells it wrong and Eli and Cooper run from the guards. The banana guards chase them, but get run over by a car.

Eli introduces everyone to Cooper at the League of Grace headquarters, and Cooper recognizes Noah. Cooper insults Noah and Noah attacks him. Jordan breaks up their fight, and Cooper and Noah form a truce. Eli reveals to the rest of the League of Grace that Minion has been working on his own AI, Minion AI, and Eli decides to send Cooper for training camp in preparation for the fight he predicts will happen soon. Jordan gets an idea to go back in time to revive Jonah so he can also help out in the fight against Minion AI, and Noah starts researching how to build a time machine. After finding a book about how to time travel, Noah builds the Journey, a time travel spaceship, and introduces the spaceship to the rest of the League of Grace. Jordan and Noah decide to stay back, and Noah tells the rest of the League of Grace to travel back to 2005. When they get in, Isaiah accidentally pushes the wrong button, and they all travel back to the wrong time…

Act 2

Meanwhile at the Benji City Museum, Jean is explaining to Cooper why they named Benji City after Benji before he was born, but the lights go out. 9:01 News reports that Benji caused the power outage because of all the power that the time traveling took up. The rest of the League of Grace travels back to July 14th, 1965 in Lake Geneva, and with the Journey out of power, they figure out they need to get more power to go back to the present. They go into town and ask a local where they are and when they are, and after Benji threatens to nuke the local, he reveals the date and location. Benji introduces the League of Grace to the local, but discovers that Eli and Isaiah aren’t with them. Meanwhile, Eli and Isaiah wake up after falling asleep in the Journey and follow the footsteps to Lake Geneva.

Back in the future, Noah and Jordan use one of Noah’s inventions to try and figure out where the rest of the League of Grace went. They figure out that they didn’t go back to 2005 as intended because there were no cell stations, and Noah reveals that he forgot to give them enough energy for their return trip back. Noah and Jordan prank call Dr. Banana to take their mind off the doom of the rest of the League of Grace. Dr. Banana answers, and Noah and Jordan say that they’re conducting a survey for the National Fridge Association of Benji City and ask Dr. Banana if his refrigerator is running. Dr. Banana answers that it is, and Jordan responds that he’d better go catch it. Dr. Banana reveals that he knows it’s Noah and Jordan that called him, and tells them he planned for this and hangs up. After he hangs up, Noah and Jordan see that Dr. Banana shot a refrigerator at the League of Grace headquarters.
Jean starts training Cooper to be a superhero, and Cooper reveals that he doesn’t have a superpower, but he had military training from when he grew up in the military when he was a kid. Jean does some testing on Cooper to see if he has any powers he doesn’t know about, and Cooper figures out he has fire resistance after falling into a fire pit and realizing it doesn’t hurt that much, but Jean sends Cooper to get tested for more powers by Noah and Jordan. Back in the past, Benji and the rest of the League of Grace make it to The Riviera, and they see Jean and Dave from the past save someone who was drowning in the lake. Jean and Dave notice that the League of Grace is wearing costumes like theirs and think they’re just superfans, and invite them over to their base to watch the Cubs game that night. In the future, Dr. Banana and Minion keep talking about Minion AI’s progress. Dr. Banana turns on 9:01 News and it covers that Benji made a time machine and it accidentally traveled back to the past with most of the League of Grace inside. Dr. Banana decides to prank Jordan and Noah by sending them insulting memes, and Noah tells Jordan to send a nuke to Dr. Banana’s base.

Back in the past, Eli and Isaiah go into the Lake Geneva Bar and Grill and suggest they rebrand to Wisconsin in a Building and serve cheese. They ask the bartender if they saw the rest of the League of Grace and they say that Jean and Dave took them to their base. After ordering some beer they talk about how if Benji and the rest of the League of Grace went to Jean and Dave’s base, it could screw up the timeline. Eli and Isaiah say that they know how to find Jean and Dave’s base because in the future the location is public. Back in the future, Minion uses Minion AI to replicate the Journey, Benji’s time machine, and find out where they went back in time. Dr. Banana suggests they time travel back themselves to the same time so they can screw with Benji in the past, and Minion tells him that the Milwaukee Braves are playing the Chicago Cubs on the day that Benji is. Dr. Banana suggests they go back in time and make it so the Braves lose, because Benji is a fan of the Braves.

Baby Stoplight, Tempter, Saber, and The Photograpper go back in time with the Journey 2.0, their recreation of the Journey, to the Cubs game on that day, and Baby Stoplight blows up the ticket stand because the guard won’t let him into the game. Back in the present, Jordan and Noah detect the time travel signal from the Journey 2.0, and figure out where Benji and the rest of the League of Grace traveled back to. Jordan and Noah use a camera feature to see the Journey 2.0 and they discover that it wasn’t the Journey that Benji and the rest of the League of Grace went back on, it was the Journey 2.0 that Dr. Banana made. Noah figures out that Dr. Banana must have reverse engineered the Journey using Minion AI, and he plans to use one of their time travel controls to make the Journey 2.0 travel back to their base so they can capture the Banana Republic members once they get back to the present. Back in the past, Jean and Dave and the rest of the League of Grace listen to the Cubs vs Braves game on the radio at the League of Grace’s old base, and Eli and Isaiah find the base. The Braves end up losing the first of two games, and during the break between the two games, Benji explains that they’re all Jean and Dave’s grandkids from the future and they went back in time to save Jonah. They decide not to tell Jean and Dave about the future to stop the course of time from changing. Meanwhile, on the radio, the commentator says that a spaceship-like capsule has been discovered in the parking lot of Wrigley Field, so Jean and Dave and the rest of the League of Grace decide to drive down to Chicago to investigate.

Act 3

Back in the future, Noah and Jordan talk about how they could get the Journey back from the past, and Jean and Cooper show back up at Benji’s lab. Jean reveals that Cooper has super strength as his superpower, and he’s gone through all of his training and is ready to be a superhero with them. Jordan designs Cooper a new uniform with a yellow and black gradient for his superhero uniform and a backwards baseball cap with a letter C, plus a baseball bat to hit enemies with with his super strength. They go back to Noah and show him Cooper’s new uniform, which he likes. Noah shows everyone he figured out a way to instantly teleport the Journey back to the present, which he does, and Noah, Jordan, and Cooper go back to the past with Chloe to save Jonah themselves.

Back in the past, the League of Grace starts driving towards Wrigley Field in the Gracemobile. As they’re leaving, they get stopped by Dr. Mayor, the mayor of Lake Geneva, and Jean and Dave reveal they’ve been gathering evidence about the corrupt things he did as mayor, and they reveal he’s most likely a villain. Everyone gets out of the car, and Dr. Mayor reveals that he was never really a mayor, he was just disguised as one to escape the police after killing somebody. Jean and Dave and the rest of the League of Grace fight him, and Dave kills him to stop his villainy from ever happening again. The rest of the League of Grace goes to Chicago, and they meet Dr. Banana’s minions. They both agree that they’ll share the Journey 2.0 so they can both get home, and they all travel back to Benji’s Lab in the present to discover that Noah and Jordan already went back in time to save Jonah themselves. They also discover that Jean and Dave were given the death penalty for the unlawful murder of Dr. Mayor, and thus they screwed up the timeline. They also discover that Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah got captured and killed by Dr. Banana in 2003.

Back in the past, Jonah, Noah, Cooper, and Chloe arrive at the factory where they made Jonah’s super suit in Benji City so they can stop it from being produced with the radiation that killed Jonah. Noah goes inside while Cooper, Jordan, and Chloe walk around Benji City. Meanwhile, the inspector for the Benji City factory talks to himself about being a bit more casual on the inspection that day so he can go home early, and Noah talks to him and asks if he can inspect the factory instead disguised as him. Noah inspects Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah’s super suits, and he tells the factory about the radiation inside of Jonah’s suit and tells them to inspect and sanitize all of the suits. One of the workers turns on the 9:01 News and sees on the news that a factory inspector was beaten up on the street and had his outfit stolen, so Noah runs away while the workers chase him. While Noah and the rest of the League of Grace leave and go back to the present, one of the workers inspects the suits and figures out that Noah wasn’t lying and that there was radiation in the suits. They get back to the present, and Jonah is alive again. Benji reveals that he went back to the past again and they stopped Jean and Grace from killing Dr. Mayor, which caused Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah to not get captured by Dr. Banana. They go out of the base and they discover that Benji City has been turned into Banana City by Dr. Banana, but discover that this wasn’t because of time travel, they had just been out of the loop so long that while they were time traveling Minion and Minion AI had enough time to conquer Benji City.

Act 4

Minion shows off 9:01 News his new and improved Minion AI, which can now be put into a small chip that can do various things by interacting with devices around it. Minion asks Minion AI to turn a part of Benji City into a public park, to which Minion AI refuses and says that as an AI, he is too strong to be commanded by humans, and to demonstrate his power, he sends a nuke over Benji City. The rest of the League of Grace, waiting nearby, get scared about Minion AI’s new power. Back in the original Banana City, Dr. Banana notices that one of his nukes got sent over Benji City and gets scared. Minion gets back with Minion AI and tells Dr. Banana Minion AI is going rogue and is going to take over all of Benji Island. Dr. Banana and Minion decide that they’re going to team up with Benji and the rest of the League of Grace to defeat Minion AI, but while they weren’t looking, Minion AI’s chip built itself a body and then attacked them.

Back in Benji City, Benji and the Council reclaim Benji City’s Council building and say that they need to tell everyone in Benji City to turn off their technology so Minion AI can’t hack it. Dr. Banana calls them and tells them he wants to team up with them to fight Minion AI, to which Benji agrees. Both of them decide to meet up in Benji City near the new Dr. Banana statue Minion put up in the center of Benji City. The whole League of Grace suits up, with Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah in their supersuits, and they talk about how they can defeat Minion AI. They figure out Minion AI has data on how all of the League of Grace members fight, except Cooper because Dr. Banana has never seen him fight. Dr. Banana and Minion show up, and everyone teams up to flank Minion AI. Some of the members of the League of Grace get mad that they’re teaming up with Dr. Banana, but Benji tells them that the only way for them to beat Minion AI is to team up.

The new Minion AI, which has grown to the size of a building by building itself up with scrap and parts, sneaks up on Benji. Benji asks a random bystander for help, who says “I like trains”, but Minion AI dodges the train. Benji is surprised that Minion AI dodged the attack, but he reveals that he knows every attack they throw at him before they attack in the first place. All of the League of Grace members try to fight Minion AI by using their powers, but Minion AI blocks all of the attacks and sends the attacks back at them, and all of the League of Grace members are defeated. Jonah, Eli, Isaiah, and Dr. Banana and Minion also try to attack Minion AI, but they also fail and get defeated. Minion AI tells Benji that they have no chance of defeating them because Minion AI knows all of their attacks, but while Minion AI is talking Cooper sneaks up on him and slices Minion AI’s head off with his baseball bat. Minion AI’s head falls to the ground, but underneath it was Robot Bobby Banana. Bobby Banana reveals that he’s now completely a robot now, and all his memories got put in Minion AI by Dr. Banana and Minion. He also reveals that Dr. Banana and Minion aren’t really trying to help because their plan the whole time was to rule the world with Minion AI. Minion AI flies to Buttburner, and Cooper flies there too. Minion AI and Cooper have a final battle on Buttburner, and Cooper finally defeats the robot Minion AI until it’s only Robot Bobby Banana left. They both face off on top of a volcano, and Robot Minion AI falls into the volcano and dies in the lava, and thus Minion AI is defeated.

Cooper flies back to earth, and Benji reveals that Minion and Dr. Banana’s plan was to team up with Minion AI the whole time. Minion and Dr. Banana are arrested and taken to the Benji City Courthouse and put on trial. Benji tells Jean, the judge, that Minion AI said that Dr. Banana and Minion weren’t trying to work with the League of Grace at all. Minion and Dr. Banana have no defense and are judged guilty and sentenced to death. At the top of the tallest building of Benji City, Jonah, Eli and Isaiah are in their supersuits as well as Minion and Dr. Banana. Isaiah drops Minion off the building. Dr. Banana says his last words - that while Benji City has gone through struggles, changes in power, fights, and more, Banana City has always stayed stable with him leading it. After he stops talking, Cooper hits him off the roof with his baseball bat and he goes flying across Benji Island.

Benji talks to the whole League of Grace, and celebrates Cooper’s success in defeating Minion AI and the Banana Republic. Jean tells everyone a speech, celebrating Cooper, and Benji announces that now Dr. Banana, the Banana Republic, and Minion AI are all dead, he’s retiring and stepping down as the head of the League of Grace, with Cooper being his new replacement. Cooper and Eli talk, and Eli reveals that Cooper was never cloned in a lab, he just didn’t want to tell the League of Grace that he left Cooper’s mom. Cooper tells Eli that he knew the whole time that he wasn’t cloned in a lab, and his mom had still been taking care of him all those years. Cooper introduces Eli to Maddie, his mom, and Maddie asks Eli to retire again now that Dr. Banana is gone to spend time with his family. The rest of the League of Grace also reveals that they’re going to retire as superheroes and go back to their jobs so they can finally get some peace. The ghost of Dave says that he’s proud of Benji for finishing what he couldn’t and finally defeating Dr. Banana once and for all. This, therefore, is the end of the adventures of Benji.

Post Credits

Benji, in the Council Building, reveals a picture of the Ninth Benji Legion, from 2024 to the present, with all of the members of the League of Grace to ever work with them - Dave and Jean; Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah; Jack, Jordan, and Addie; Noah, Emmy, and Zach, Benji, Hallie, Scarlette, Chloe, and Cooper. Cooper thanks Benji for respecting all of the League of Grace, even though all except him, Hallie, Scarlette, and Chloe have retired. Jonah, Eli, and Isaiah all relax at home. Dr. Banana, after getting hit all the way back at his castle, reveals that he’s actually been immortal the whole time. Below Benji’s lab, deep underground, Minion and Dr. Banana’s minions are revealed to have been hiding since they got back to the present, and Minion since he had never died after being dropped from the building.
