Chapter 1: Banana

Act 1 - Banana: Episode 1 (Pilot)

In the Banana Enterprises power plant on January 19th, 1966, two workers work on making guns inside the factory. They complain about the fact that they have to make guns at what’s supposed to just be a banana plantation, but their boss, Dr. Mayor, overhears the two workers, Jerry and Nathan, talking instead of working, and whips both workers for slacking off, as well as firing Nathan and making him work in the banana farms as a slave. While Nathan works at the banana farms, he takes one of the bananas for himself and kicks it out of anger, and it lands inside a pit of radioactive waste inside of the factory.

Bryce, another factory worker standing above the waste, notices that the banana fell in, and sounds the alarm that a banana fell into the waste. Dr. Mayor talks to him and says that he’s going to a party to celebrate his rigged mayor election, and that he really doesn’t care. Bryce goes down to investigate, but a board from the ceiling falls on his head as Jean and Dave fly down from above to investigate the factory.

Jean and Dave reveal to Dr. Mayor that they had been suspicious of him ever since he rigged the mayor election, and now they’ve found his illegal operations, they’re going to arrest him. Dr. Mayor says that he’s just going to move out of Lake Geneva to an island to avoid consequences, so Jean and Dave kill him and drop him into the nuclear waste.

Jean and Dave leave the factory, but after they leave, Dr. Banana climbs out of the nuclear waste, and is already able to talk and understand English. Nathan sees Dr. Banana and tells him that he was the banana that he dropkicked into the nuclear waste, and Dr. Banana decides Nathan is his boss.

Dr. Banana tells Nathan that Dr. Mayor fell into the nuclear waste, and says that he knows that before Dr. Mayor died, he had a plan to move Banana Enterprises to a remote island in the middle of the ocean. Nathan decides that he’ll follow the plan and move Banana Enterprises to the remote island.

Meanwhile, at Jean and Dave’s base, Dave tells Jean that he’s concerned that people might figure out that they killed Dr. Mayor because of Banana Enterprises’ security cameras and eyewitnesses, but Jean reassures him that they would be the last people in mind as culprits because of all they’ve done for Lake Geneva.

They turn on the radio, and 9:01 News reveals that they know that Jean and Dave were the people who assassinated Dr. Mayor and that they also know that Dr. Mayor rigged the election and was in charge of the illegal factory. Jean and Dave decide to pack up and leave Lake Geneva to avoid public backlash, and they take their flying car and decide to fly to a deserted island in the middle of the ocean.

Meanwhile, Dr. Banana and the other Banana Enterprises workers are loading everyone onto Banana Enterprises’ ship, the S. S. Banana. Dr. Banana and Banana Enterprises sets sail for the new island…

Act 2 - Banana: Part 2 (Super Benji Web bonus material)

On the S. S. Banana, Dr. Banana talks to Nathan while he plans the route to the island, and says that his plan for Banana Enterprises is to start up a banana plantation and worker’s city there, and then export the bananas to other countries without needing to pay tax and follow laws.

Meanwhile, Jean and Dave arrive on the island and set up camp. They use the solar power on their car to power all of the other electric devices they need, and after gathering some food for dinner, they see smoke in the distance.

While walking towards the smoke, they walk through a grassy plain, a big forest with difficult terrain, through a rocky gray area with volcanoes, and even an area with red rocks and lava rivers. Eventually they walk through another plain area, where they discover the native inhabitants of the island, the Hallies, who are tall humans that range in height from six to nine feet.

Dave tries to talk to them, but the only word they say is Hallie, so Dave names them the Hallies. They keep walking and discover a camp on the other side of the island from theirs, where various workers are building a factory, using a generator which was the source of all the smoke. Other workers are planting bananas, and Dr. Banana is commanding everyone.

Dr. Banana is surprised that Jean and Dave are here, and Jean and Dave reveal that they left Lake Geneva because everyone hates them for killing Dr. Mayor. Dr. Banana tells them that the island, or at least this part, is the property of Banana Enterprises.

Jean and Dave finish their lap around the island, walking through more of the red rocky area, through a cave with lots of crystals with a rainy island in the distance, and then build a raft to cross a bay which they name the Grace Bay.

They walk across a desert which was across the bay, then to a city full of anthropomorphic acorns which speak English. A sign on the ground says “Acorn Village, Population 250”. They continue onward and walk up a range of mountains, one with a giant acorn at the top. After walking over the mountains, they walk back through the forest and grassland and end up back at their camp.

Meanwhile, the next day, the trees that Dr. Banana and Banana Enterprises planted have already grown at a rate much faster than bananas planted everywhere else. Dr. Banana asks his workers to make a laboratory, and they figure out that the island is more fertile than anywhere else in the world to grow bananas.

After some testing, they discover that the reason the island is more fertile is because the air is completely pure, and they discover an atmospheric shield covering the island blocking any toxic chemicals from getting into the air.

After a couple of days, Jean and Dave fly back to Lake Geneva to see if the heat has died down. There they discover that Dr. Banana is giving a presentation in the Riviera about the future of Banana Enterprises.

They sit in and watch, and discover that Dr. Banana is inviting all the workers to immigrate to the new island, and tells them that it’s the best place in the world to grow bananas, so they’ll make a fortune from the industry.

Dr. Banana directs everyone who wants to work for Banana Enterprises to board the ship that just docked outside, the H.M.S. Banana. Jean and Dave talk and say that they don’t trust that Dr. Banana is going to make that much money, and there’s even less of a chance that his workers are going to get paid fairly.

Jean and Dave walk out of the Riviera and, as Jean is looking at the water, she gets a flashback to how her and Dave first got superpowers.

In the flashback, Jean and Dave had already been fighting crime in Lake Geneva without powers. Dave was just very strong, and Jean was really smart.

Jean looks at radiation from the Lake Geneva area, and discovers lots of it coming from the bottom of Lake Geneva. Jean and Dave go to investigate, and Jean dives into Lake Geneva to see a giant blue diamond in the water at the bottom of the lake that seems to be emitting lots of radiation.

They take it back to their base, and discover the gem is emitting lots of radiation, but it doesn’t seem to be dangerous. They also discover that the radiation the gem emits every second could power a house for a day.

They wake up the next morning with both of them being able to fly, and they discover an engraving on the gem that has a date, September 17th 2017, and a name, Benji.

Back in the present, as Jean and Dave are flying back to the island, Jean suggests they name the island Benji Island after the engraving on the gem, and Dave agrees.

Meanwhile, the H.M.S. Banana arrives on Benji Island, and Dr. Banana addresses the new arrivals. He tells the crowd that everyone will work from 8 AM to 3 PM every day, and in exchange they’ll get free food, housing, and other necessities, as well as money for everything else they might need, to which everybody cheers.

Dr. Banana divides the crowd into four groups, one that will work in the power plant section of the factory, one that will work at the plantation and farm bananas, one that will work at the factory to make equipment and firearms, and one that will work on building a city around the factory for the workers to live in.

Dr. Banana reveals that he’s also looking for people to work as administrators. A couple volunteers step up, including a potato named Minion, a star named Saber, a stoplight named Baby Stoplight, a weird cylinder thing named The Tempter, and a purple blob named The Photograpper.

Minion is given the role of Dr. Banana’s assistant and the other four are assigned to manage the four groups of workers. Everybody works on their tasks, and then goes to their new homes and settles in on their new home in Banana City.
